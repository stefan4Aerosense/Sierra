# AeroNode RV8 rounded concept V2

Exterior styling revision to restore the AeroNode rounded rectangular silhouette. Separate concept document; original AeroNode preserved.

- Body envelope: 105 x 132 x 44 mm, excluding raised logo and hidden mounting tabs.
- Outer corner radius 7 mm; upper perimeter fillet 1.5 mm.
- Teal perimeter and full-width upper cover, pearl service base and original logo geometry.
- Internal thermal-space and support-pad allocations retained. Mounting tabs retained but hidden for presentation.
- Three replacement enclosure bodies verified solid in Fusion; F3D and STEP exported.

This is a packaging and appearance concept, not demonstrated crash protection or an approved flight recorder. Connector access, heat management, mounting, sealing and closure retention remain unresolved. Thermal allocations are conceptual and contain an existing overlap; no full clash or survivability verification performed. Materials and dimensions require engineering validation. STEP may omit mesh electronics; use the F3D for the full assembly.
