# AeroNode RV-8 protective packaging — UNVERIFIED CONCEPT
Created 24 September 2026 for discussion with the electronics and aircraft installation engineers.

Current complete AeroNode preserved inside an added enclosure. Original Fusion document unchanged; separate imported revision created. This is a space-planning mock-up, not a crash-survivable recorder design or certification evidence.

Nominal shell envelope: 132 x 105 x 42.4 mm, excluding raised logo; footprint including tabs 132 x 125 mm. This is not a demonstrated minimum size. Faceted 2 mm shell, upper plate and base; 5 mm perimeter thermal allowance; 3 mm top/bottom thermal allowances; four nominal support pads and four mounting tabs with 4.5 mm holes. Dimensions are provisional; no structural, thermal, fatigue, fire or impact sizing performed. Appearances are visual only, not material specifications.

CRITICAL OPEN ITEMS
- Identify memory and recording architecture. Consider a dedicated qualified protected memory unit rather than protecting the full board.
- Existing USB/Ethernet connectors are enclosed. The current concept intentionally has no finished external electrical interface; a qualified bulkhead/harness design and its thermal/pressure sealing are unresolved. Do not build or operate this closed enclosure as shown.
- Power dissipation and battery details unknown. Thermal insulation conflicts with normal cooling; no thermal management solution has been verified.
- Tabs, lid/base retention, seals and positive payload retention are unfinished. Tabs overlap base as conceptual attachment geometry, not a qualified joint.
- Support pads are placeholders, not specified shock isolators. PCB solder joints and stacked-board retention have not been analysed.
- Outer teal styling does not address recorder identification/locatability requirements.
- Installation location, aircraft loads, wiring, fire environment, required recording functions and certification basis must be agreed with the relevant approval authority and qualified engineers.
- No crash, crush, penetration, fire, immersion or pressure test has been performed. Geometry solids were checked; no comprehensive interference or clearance qualification.
