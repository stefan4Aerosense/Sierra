## Accuracy limitations
PCB outline 15 x 20.4 mm inferred approximately from PDF drawing scale, not a dimensioned manufacturer outline. PCB thickness 0.8 mm assumed. Component positions manually digitized approximately. Connector envelope, small LED envelopes, passive/IC heights and decorative optical windows simplified/estimated. Solder, routed copper, vias and accurate silkscreen omitted. Bottom placement mirrored into top coordinate system. Do not finalize cushion tooling or fabrication using these dimensions.
