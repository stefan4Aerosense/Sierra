ABS hinged fabric clamp prototype - 7 October 2026
Added option C below existing A/B concepts in H1 - Adjustable Fabric Band - Two Concepts. Existing options preserved. Closed, 70-degree open and exploded examples are separate static components. Each includes ABS base, ABS lid, metal pivot and sample webbing.
Before-Clip-Addition.f3d preserves prior design. Completed F3D/STEP contain all concepts without external dependencies. Separate base/lid STEP and millimetre STL exports are prototype parts.
Nominal channel width 22 mm for 20 x 1.2 mm webbing. Base width 30 mm; clip height up to 14 mm at hinge; projecting mounting tab is a provisional interface. 2 mm x 31 mm metal pivot in 2.4 mm bores. Pin retention must be provided when assembled; plain pin model has no retainer. Separate pivot avoids a printed living hinge.
Rounded cylindrical ribs contact nominal fabric top at 4.2 mm. The CAD depicts nominal contact, not validated clamping force. Actual fabric compression and printed tolerances may require rib-height adjustment. Front hook catch requires flexing outward to release; latch fatigue/strain is unvalidated. ABS print orientation and temperature affect performance. Print base on its flat underside; orient lid with broad top on bed and evaluate supports/hinge bore cleanup in slicer. Do not print the example fabric or metal pin as plastic.
Closed-state temporary-solid checks found zero overlap for base/lid, base/pin, lid/pin, base/webbing and lid/webbing. This does not simulate deformation, grip or latch operation. Test closure, release, sustained slip and repeated cycles before using on the headset. Injection-moulded release requires separate draft, wall and tooling review.
