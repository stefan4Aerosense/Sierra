# AeroNode compact pouch case concept
New separate COMPACT components preserve the previous NOVA case hidden. GRP9161115 sample moved beneath electronics; other payload transforms retained. Battery is labelled 3.2 V 5 Ah, not 6 Ah; sample geometry is not a supplier-qualified drawing.

Outer envelope 92 x 138 x 53.5 mm, excluding screw heads/logo. Previous envelope 146 x 116 x 50 mm. Approximately 19.8% lower bounding volume. Transparent 2 mm shell, 0.8 mm roof-edge fillet, pearl bottom service cover, raised carrier with PCB posts and IMU clips, battery support pad allowance, four reused M2.5x8 closure screws at new corner axes. Ports follow the current board height. Existing AeroVault capsule retained.

Validated new bodies are solid and battery has zero solid interference with the four new components before roof fillet; roof-only fillet removes material. About 1.17 mm between battery envelope and carrier underside; this is a geometric allowance, not a validated swelling allowance. Port plug accessibility, thermal performance, battery restraint/swelling, carrier retention, PCB fasteners, capsule anchorage/feedthroughs and threaded inserts remain engineering tasks. No crash qualification established.

Before-change and final F3D archives retained; separate shell/base/carrier STEP files. Linked electronics dependencies are not verified self-contained.
