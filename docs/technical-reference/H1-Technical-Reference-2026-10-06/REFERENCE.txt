H1 TECHNICAL REFERENCE
Archived 6 October 2026 from C:\Users\Stefan\Documents\Aerosense\H1 - Technical Documents

PURPOSE
Manufacturer source library for future H1 electronics work, especially the A2B audio transport and ADAU1860 codec/DSP. These are capabilities and implementation references, not evidence that the parts have been selected, purchased or validated for H1. Review covers document scope, architecture, principal constraints, evaluation hardware and errata; it is not a line-by-line verification of every register or schematic.

ARCHIVE
originals/ contains all 12 source PDFs, unchanged. text/ contains page-marked full text for searching. provenance.json records file sizes, page counts and SHA-256 hashes verified against the source. PAGE-INDEX.txt provides page-level retrieval clues. Original PDFs remain authoritative for diagrams, table alignment, equations and electrical conditions. Referenced software, design ZIPs and external documents are not included. This is a dated snapshot, not a continuously updated vendor library.

DOCUMENT GUIDE
1. ad2426_27_28-silicon-anomalies.pdf — 10 pages, NR004699H, April 2026 footer; revision-history entry 19 March 2026. Read first when implementing AD2426/27/28. Page 2 maps 14 anomalies to silicon revisions 0.0–0.3; pages 3–10 supply conditions and workarounds. A2B_VERSION identifies revision. Mixed part families require each relevant errata sheet.
   Important IDs: 18000027 PRBS false errors; 28 bus monitor enable; 30 I2C clamp; 44 I2C tLOW; 50 upstream corruption during partial rediscovery; 51 main-node channel shift on first subordinate disconnect; 52 GPIO-over-distance concurrent with remote access; 59 simultaneous local/bus read; 60 invalid I2C protocol; 68 access at discovery completion; 69 PDM specification correction; 73 simultaneous writes; 75 missing NACK/I2CERR in certain mixed revisions; 76 local access around PLL lock during discovery.
   18000069 (p8) corrects PDM A-weighted SNR from 120 dB to 108 dB and identifies THD versus THD+N labeling. Applies to all four revisions; no workaround.
   18000073 (p9) applies to 0.0–0.2: coordinate local/remote writers, read back configuration, use mailbox CRC/status checks as described. 18000075 workaround is main-node rev 0.2/0.3 for the stated scenario. 18000076 (p10) applies through 0.3: avoid concurrent discovery/local access or implement the specified delay and response-cycle initialization. Do not assume newest listed silicon eliminates all issues.

2. ad2426w-2427w-2428w.pdf — 2-page Rev Sp0 May 2018 overview, NOT the full electrical datasheet. Single differential pair carries synchronous PCM, clocks, control and optional bus power. AD2428W can be main/subordinate. Describes I2S/TDM, I2C over distance, GPIO, PDM microphones, up to 32 channels each direction. Stated 15 m adjacent/40 m total limits are from this old overview; later documents describe other limits. Do not mix limits without checking supported topology and full current datasheet.

3. ad2428-automotive-safety-application-note.pdf — 4 pages, Rev04 March 2024. Describes ISO26262 proven-in-use argument for automotive W-grade shipment population. Explicitly says product was developed as QM, not to assigned ASIL safety requirements. Integrator assesses applicability. This does not establish aviation certification or H1 system compliance. p1 describes 44.1/48 kHz superframes, SYSBCLK 1024 times frame rate.

4. ad242x-trm.pdf — 283 PDF pages, technical reference Rev1.4 January 2024. Detailed source for bus architecture, I2C access/latencies, PDM, I2S/TDM, mailboxes, discovery, slot routing, interrupts/diagnostics and register programming. Use chapter/page labels inside original; PAGE-INDEX uses PDF page numbers. Read with silicon errata, not in isolation. Audio/control/power sharing does not by itself establish an H1 power or fail-safe budget.

5. adau1860.pdf — 30 pages, Rev C. Three ADCs and ONE differential DAC output; not a two-channel stereo DAC. FastDSP plus Tensilica HiFi 3z, 288 kB memory, clocks 24.576/49.152/73.728/98.304 MHz, up to 768 kHz audio modes. Eight digital mic inputs, two serial audio ports, ASRC, interpolation/decimation, QSPI self-boot and JTAG. p1 specifies 5 us analog-to-analog delay only at 768 kHz with FastDSP bypass/zero instructions. It is not full ANC, network or headset latency. p3 block diagram visually checked.
   p1 ranges: IOVDD 1.1–1.98 V, DVDD 0.85–1.21 V; HPVDD typical 1.8 V; HPVDD_L 1.2 V to HPVDD. Do not directly assume 3.3 V host compatibility. WLCSP 56 balls, 0.35 mm pitch, 2.980 x 2.679 mm; layout/assembly capability matters.
   Document states ADAU1860 ordering for PRC customers, ADAU1860-1 outside PRC with shared features. Confirm exact orderable part with supplier when procuring; no availability checked here.

6. adau186x-hardware-reference-manual-ug-2257.pdf — 337 pages, Rev0. Family manual for ADAU1860 and ADAU1861; distinguish package/power naming by exact part. Reading map: p12–16 power/clock initialization and multichip phase synchronization; p17 routing; p18–21 inputs/ADC/digital microphones; p22–24 outputs, ASRC, rate conversion and levels; p25 EQ; p26–28 FastDSP, banks and SafeLoad; p29 turbo; p30 Tensilica/DMA/FIFO/peripherals; p31 memory map; p32 power saving; p34–40 control interfaces; p41 self-boot; p42 multipurpose pins; p43–44 serial ports; p45 application guidance; remaining pages detailed registers, including DMA p47, QSPI p59, SOC p81, UART p87, timers p96, watchdog p99, datapath p102 and FIFO/memory controls near end. Consult original tables before writing register values.

7. EE419v01.pdf — 10 pages, Rev1 October 2019, EVAL-AD2428WB1BZ. Bus-powered subordinate board, ADAU1761 stereo codec/SigmaDSP, two PDM microphones, EEPROM, GPIO LED/button. J7 A-side toward main; J8 B-side toward downstream. External I2S/TDM header and analog I/O permit bench integration. Refers to separate hardware design ZIP not in archive. SigmaStudio4.4/A2B19.3.0 are historical minimum versions in this guide.

8. EE420v01.pdf — 7 pages, Rev1 October 2019, EVAL-AD2428WC1BZ. Bus-powered microphone board with four INMP621 microphones, GPIO LED and probe points. Useful source for microphone-array acquisition; not a headset-output evaluation board. Body clarifies four microphones despite ambiguous feature-list wording. Same J7/J8 topology and historical software requirements.

9. EE421v01.pdf — 9 pages, Rev1 October 2019, EVAL-AD2428WG1BZ. Locally powered subordinate, ADAU1761 codec, LT3505/LT1965 supplies, EEPROM, GPIO and I2S/TDM access. Can use A2B bias as isolated power-enable signal. Supplied 12 V adapter; terminal input limits are board-specific. Do not substitute regulator absolute capability for board input rating. External design ZIP is separate.

10. ee459v01.pdf — 32 pages, Rev1 September 2024. AD2428 audio-performance/jitter measurements. Recovered-clock jitter accumulates across nodes even when data are bit-correct. Covers TIE versus cycle/period jitter, converter measurements and spread spectrum. Conclusion p29: results depend strongly on converter, level and clocking; low-bandwidth PLL, jitter attenuator or ASRC/local clean clock can mitigate degradation. Spread-spectrum effects were small in measured configurations, not a universal result for H1. For phase-critical DVNC, any added clock/rate conversion also needs a latency and coherence budget. Intro describes up to 80 m, unlike old two-page overview; reconcile revisions before use. Header date September26 differs from document-history September20; preserve both source dates.

11. EVAL-AD2428WD1BZ_Manual.pdf — 40 pages, Rev1.1 October2019. Main-node evaluation platform with AD2428, ADAU1452 DSP, ADAU1761 codec, EEPROM and regulators. Source for default configuration, switches/jumpers, power connections, serial audio access and test points. Appropriate reference for the main end of a bench link to WB/WC/WG; not a ready-made H1 production controller. Check actual board revision against manual before powering or changing jumpers.

12. eval-adau1860-ug-2017.pdf — 26 pages, Rev A November2022. EVAL-ADAU1860EBZ with mIDAS-Link; Lark Studio and SDK for control/code, JTAG for HiFi3z debugging. USB or external5V board power is regulated internally; it is not the bare-chip supply voltage. Onboard24.576MHz or external clock. Reading map: p4 setup/default jumpers; p5–8 basic signal flow; p9 power; p10 control; p11 codec/I/O; p12 serial/other interfaces; p13–14 jumpers; p15–23 schematics/artwork; p24–25 BOM. Toolchain/license access must be confirmed independently; possession of PDF does not establish access.

RELATION TO EXISTING AERONODE REFERENCES
A2B adds a concrete synchronous digital-audio transport candidate to the earlier CAN/RS485/UWB discussion; it does not prove an architecture change was approved. ADAU1860 was a candidate in the July Audio Core document; these sources allow detailed evaluation. Separate local fast ANC, networked audio, DVNC control/reference timing, sidetone and fail-safe analog communication paths. Define one-versus-two codec arrangement for stereo H1, clock master, routing, microphone allocation, power consumption, connector/pinout and reconnection behavior before schematic freeze.
