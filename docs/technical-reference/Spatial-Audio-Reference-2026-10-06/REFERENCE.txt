WHAT IT COVERS
1. Motivation: supplementary nonvisual cues in degraded visual environments, spatial disorientation and workload. Distinguish communications separation, directional threat warnings, navigation cues and continuous attitude sonification: evidence for one does not automatically validate another.
2. Psychoacoustics: interaural timing for lower-frequency azimuth, intensity/level differences for higher-frequency azimuth, pinna spectral cues for elevation/front-back discrimination. Source uses approximate boundaries of 1.5 kHz and 4 kHz. HRTF convolution creates binaural sources; externalization and individual anatomy are important. Distance cues include level, spectrum and direct/reverberant relationship.
3. Sensor chain: aircraft AHRS/IMU supplies orientation, head tracking updates rendering. Discusses gyroscope/accelerometer/magnetometer fusion and integration with air data. Embedded equations describe accelerometer-based tilt. Their applicability under maneuver acceleration needs specific review before implementation; no flight-ready estimator is established here.
4. Reference frames: earth-fixed cues versus aircraft-fixed cues versus head-fixed presentation. Maintaining a world-stable auditory horizon needs explicit frame definitions, head-to-aircraft alignment, sign conventions and synchronized updates. Document discusses inside-out/outside-in attitude mapping.
5. Research examples: historical De Florez auditory bank cues, simulated helicopter DVE navigation, aircraft-referenced audio flight study, NASA-TLX workload and spatial release from masking. Bibliography mixes primary studies, manufacturer brochures, patents and secondary reporting; strength of evidence varies.
6. Product/patent examples: Terma military spatial audio; Garmin communications/avionics examples; Boeing situational-awareness headset US20230252730A1 and claimed grant US12475654B2; Qualcomm US12120498B2; Bose EP3346729A1; Honeywell EP2434470; US5486821A artificial-horizon warning. These are research leads, not a freedom-to-operate conclusion or verified claim mapping. Some examples concern visual or maintenance systems rather than auditory attitude guidance.
7. Hardware discussion: broad frequency response, low distortion, ANC/passive isolation, stable HRTF reproduction and communications fallback. Source presents Terma-related figures of 20 Hz–16 kHz, less than 0.5% distortion, up to 48 kHz sample rate. A separate table lists under 3 degrees azimuth / 10 degrees elevation, 22–48 kHz, under 50 ms motion-to-sound, 20–30 dB attenuation and approximately 512 MB storage. These are source assertions, not accepted H1 design targets.
8. Human factors: workload, visual fixation, communication masking, sensory illusions, training and failure behavior. Claims of 15% fatal mishaps, 80% situational-awareness agreement and 1.5-second reaction improvement require examination of original study populations and conditions before external use.

CONNECTION TO AEROSENSE
Most directly relevant to VGSA: AeroNode supplies aircraft attitude, H1 supplies relative head orientation and binaural reproduction. Complements the July Audio Core reference and the H1 ADAU1860/A2B source library. It does not change selected hardware or implement a new feature.
Keep separate requirements for local ANC latency, audio transport latency, sidetone and complete motion-to-sound latency. The under-50-ms table here and under-1-ms language in the earlier Audio Core document are not interchangeable validated budgets. Establish sensor acquisition, fusion, transport, buffering, HRTF processing and DAC contributions explicitly.
A2B synchronization and ADAU1860 multichip synchronization are relevant candidate capabilities, not proof of complete system coherence. Individual HRTF fit, headset response, fit/seal variability, head-tracker drift and loss of tracking need evaluation.

ISSUES TO RESOLVE BEFORE USING AS DESIGN AUTHORITY
- Outside-in paragraph says sound moves in the direction of recovery but then gives left sound / right bank as its example. This is internally ambiguous. Do not implement control or cue signs from that sentence.
- Attitude relative to gravity is not terrain clearance. Terrain/obstacle direction requires additional data; a sensor box supplying attitude alone does not establish ground-proximity awareness.
- Source moves between communications separation, military threat cueing and attitude recovery. Preserve those distinctions when evaluating claimed benefits.
- Manufacturer performance, patent ownership/status/scope and accident statistics have not been independently checked in this archival review.
- Broad claims such as spatial audio being virtually impossible without ANC, replacing primary flight cues, or guaranteeing mitigation should not become product claims from this document alone.
- Validate loss/stale data handling and communications priority; an incorrect stable cue can be misleading even when sound quality is excellent.
